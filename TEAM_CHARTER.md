# 📜 Group 71 - Team Charter & Governance Rules
## 🗳️ Decision-Making Rules

- **Consensus First**: Team members should aim for consensus on major decisions.  
- **Majority Vote**: If consensus cannot be reached within 24 hours, decisions are made by simple majority vote.  
- **Pod Lead Tie-Breaker**: In case of a tie, the Pod Lead has the deciding vote.  
- **Documentation**: All decisions must be documented in the project board or issue tracker for transparency.
